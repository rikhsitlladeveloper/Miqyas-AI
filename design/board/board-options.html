Three ways to design the Board

All three show the same six jobs: two being measured, one estimate, one quote waiting for an answer, one wall being built and one finished. The Board answers "where is each job, and what do I do next?". It stays on this phone only.

Side-scrolling columnsFive stage columns at phone width: you see one and a bit, never the whole picture.
Moving takes three tapsMove, then a sheet, then pick the stage. Most moves are just "next stage".
"0 of 3 done" hides the jobCards count tasks but don't say what the next task is.
Repeated chips and notesStage chips above the columns repeat the column headers, and the "phone only" note takes a row every visit.
A

Stage list, top to bottom

A five-step strip shows the count at each stage. Below, each stage is a short list: the job, the customer and the next task. A "Next" button moves a job on with one tap. Finished jobs fold away.

B

One column at a time, swipe

Still a kanban, but each stage fills the screen. Swipe or tap a stage to change column. Cards are bigger: the task checklist, the latest note and a "Next stage" button sit on the card.
