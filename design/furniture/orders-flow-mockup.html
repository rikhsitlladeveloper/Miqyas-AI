Furniture order flow

Version 2: a three-field new order, “What do they need?”, furniture designed from sizes alone, and putting it into a room later. Everything in the phone is clickable; the designer itself and the laser are simulated.

Try it

  1. Tap +. Only name, phone and address. Buyurtma yaratish.
  2. Nima kerak? → pick Shkaf (or Ikkalasi). The order renames itself.
  3. Faqat mebel o‘lchamlari → a gallery of ready models with 3D pictures (kitchens: straight, L, U, island, peninsula; wardrobes: 2- and 3-door hinged, 2- and 3-door sliding, open shelves). Pick one, its sizes are filled in to start; change them → Loyihalashni boshlash.
  4. Back on the order: the Xonasiz mebel card. Tap Xonaga qo‘yish → Yangi xonani o‘lchash, press the laser for A, B and the height, then pick the wall. Try a wall that is too short to see the red notice.
  5. Open the wardrobe again and tap Xonadan chiqarish to take it back out.
  6. For a kitchen, pick Xonani o‘lchash to see the full room-first path.

For your OK

  • Order name starts as the customer's name and becomes “Shkaf — Akmal” when the type is chosen (unless you renamed it).
  • Room or sizes is asked for each piece; with Ikkalasi the kitchen comes first and the wardrobe waits in the next-step card.
  • Placing: a kitchen's first side goes on the wall you pick, starting in its left corner, the next side on the next wall round; a wardrobe goes on one wall, in the corner or a typed distance from it.
  • Doesn't fit: “Devorga moslash” shrinks it to the wall (or ceiling); “O‘lchamni saqlash” keeps it and marks it.
  • Prices here are simple samples (3,3 mln per running metre, 2,6 mln per metre of wardrobe); the app uses the Narx tab.
