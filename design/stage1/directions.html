Directions

Every phone is drawn at 360 × 760 pt, a common small Android size, so tight layouts show up now rather than on a device. In the room editor, wall B has a simulated laser reading that is received but not yet confirmed. Wall A is confirmed. Swipe each row sideways on a phone.

  • Risk: glare and reflections make dark screens hard to read outdoors and in bright rooms.

  • Risk: lime on dark green is a common look and would need more work to feel like its own.

Recommendation: B, Ruletka

Building sites are bright, gloves are thick and phones are held at arm's length. B leads on all three criteria that matter there.

  • Readability. It has the highest contrast and the largest numerals. The condensed family absorbs long labels such as «Подтвердить измерение» without shrinking the text.
  • State clarity. Selected, received and confirmed each get their own shape: fill, stripes or a check icon. They stay distinguishable in grayscale, in glare and with color-blindness.
  • Report fit. Black on white prints cleanly, so the PDF can use the same type and stripe language.

One caveat from testing: B's typeface needs a font fix before Uzbek Cyrillic looks right (see its risks). If you'd rather avoid a modified font, Roboto Flex keeps the same layout with plainer letters.

Two things are worth taking from A for Stage 2. The PDF should use A's numbered room schedule and ledger double rule, which is how builders' paper estimates already look. On the canvas, the hatched walls are better than solid black once rooms have columns and recesses. C's palette can come back later as an optional dark theme for interiors. It is not in the pilot scope.

Product rules that shape the design

Taken from PRODUCT_SPEC.md and BUILD_PLAN.md. Each one ends with what it means for the interface.

Measure, review, confirm

The builder selects a dimension first, then takes a reading, reviews it and confirms it. A distance never decides which wall it belongs to.

Every dimension has a visible state: unset, received or confirmed. Rectangle templates add a fourth, derived from the opposite wall. Cancelling leaves the old value.

Manual entry always works

Typing a value needs no Bluetooth permission and no device.

Manual entry stays one tap away in every measurement state, including while waiting for the device.

Simulated data is labelled

Manual, simulated and device readings are separate sources. Hardware support is unverified.

Every simulated reading carries a "Simulyatsiya" label. The copy never claims a laser model works until it has been tested.

Interior dimensions define the room

Wall thickness is entered separately. An opening has a wall, width, height, offset from the wall's start corner and, for windows, sill height.

The plan shows wall letters and start corners. The opening sheet shows which corner the offset is measured from.

Explain errors, never auto-fix

Self-intersecting shapes, openings that don't fit and overlaps are rejected.

The error names the problem and the change that fixes it. The app doesn't change measurements on its own. Unresolved dimensions are marked.

Floors hold separate rooms

Rooms are not joined into a floor plan. Each room has a flat ceiling and its own height.

The project screen groups rooms under floors and shows each room's height.

Quantities are the builder's

Geometry never rewrites material quantities or labor charges.

Areas appear only as read-only reference. Report quantities are labelled as entered by the builder, and the area convention is stated: openings deducted, reveals and columns excluded.

Money is exact

Each project has one currency and a set precision. Each line is rounded, then lines are summed. Negative and invalid values are rejected. A price of zero is allowed.

The numeric fields show the unit and currency, explain each error inline and accept zero.

Draft or issued

Drafts can be edited. Issuing a quote freezes a snapshot. A revision keeps every older quote.

Draft and issued look different. An issued quote opens read-only with a lock icon and can't be edited.

Two language settings

The report language is chosen separately from the interface language. There are four languages, including two Uzbek scripts.

The report preview has its own language picker. Layouts must survive long Russian labels and Cyrillic Extended letters.

Offline, no account

The whole workflow runs and saves on the device. Backup and restore work from a file.

There is no sign-in screen. A "saved on device" status replaces any cloud language.

Expiry keeps the basics

A lapsed subscription still allows measurement capture, viewing, backup and PDF export of saved work, including drafts.

A read-only mode marks locked controls with a lock icon and a reason. It never hides data or blocks export.

Out of scope, so not designed: curved walls, sloped ceilings, stairs, furniture, photo annotation, AI design, connected floor plans, shared price catalogs, automatic takeoff, cloud sync, discounts, taxes and deposits. The earlier concept boards show several of these. They are left out here on purpose.

Bottom tab

  • Builder details for reports
  • Units and number format
  • Default currency and precision
  • Measuring device, and the simulator during the pilot
  • Backup and restore
  • Subscription status

3D is a mode of the room editor, not a separate page

It's the same room, and builders switch back and forth to check openings. A toggle keeps zoom and selection. Tradeoff: 3D gets a smaller toolbar, and saving views for the report lives there.

Reports and revisions become "Takliflar" inside the project

The report preview opens from the estimate, because a report comes from an estimate. The Takliflar section lists issued quotes. Tradeoff: there's no stand-alone report entry point, so the preview is always one step from Smeta.

The room editor hides the bottom tabs

This gives the canvas about 60 pt more height, which matters most on small phones. Tradeoff: leaving the editor takes a back tap. The editor has its own measurement flow, so Quick Measure isn't needed there.

Device status is a chip, not a destination

The chip in the editor and in Quick Measure opens a device sheet. Pairing details live in Settings. The chip never covers the canvas or the input field.

Primary journey

These are the steps from opening the app to issuing a quote, using the acceptance fixture. The Stage 2 prototype follows these steps.

  1. Open the app. On first run, choose the interface language and optionally enter builder details. There is no account.
  2. Create a project. Enter a name, customer and address. Currency defaults to UZS with no decimals. "1-qavat" is created automatically.
  3. Add a room. Enter "Mehmonxona" and choose the rectangle template. The editor opens with the length selected.
  4. Measure. Select wall A, take a reading or type it, review it and confirm 5,00 m. Repeat for wall B (4,00 m). Set height 3,00 and wall thickness 0,20 in room properties.
  5. Add openings. Door on wall A: 0,90 × 2,10, offset 0,50. Window on wall C: 1,20 × 1,50, offset 1,00, sill 0,90.
  6. Check in 3D. Orbit, confirm the openings sit in the right walls, and save a view for the report.
  7. Build the estimate. Add paint at 12 × 50 000 and flooring at 22 × 120 000, then labor at 1 000 000. The total is 4 240 000 so‘m.
  8. Preview the report. Choose the report language, check the parties and views, then add notes.
  9. Issue the quote. Confirm that it will be frozen. Taklif №1 appears in Takliflar, and the PDF can be shared.
  10. Revise. Later edits change only the draft. Issuing again creates №2, and №1 stays unchanged.

Defaults where the spec is silent

Each of these is a proposal you can override. None blocks your choice of direction.

  • Number format follows the language. Uzbek and Russian use a decimal comma and space-separated thousands (5,00 m and 4 240 000). English uses a decimal point. Input accepts either separator.
  • Lengths in meters. Plans show two decimals, or three when the value has millimeters. The measurement sheet always shows three. Settings will offer cm and mm. Please confirm which unit your builders talk in.
  • Wall letters. Walls are lettered clockwise from the top-left corner (A, B, C, D). Each wall starts at its clockwise corner, and offsets are measured from there.
  • One estimate per project, not per room, matching the spec's single grand total.
  • Quote numbering. Quotes are numbered "Taklif №1", "№2" and so on per project. A revision is a new number, not a version suffix.
  • Light theme only for the pilot. A dark theme can follow if field use asks for it.
  • Uzbek apostrophe. The correct character in oʻ and gʻ is ʻ (U+02BB). In testing, Sofia Sans, Geologica and Golos Text don't include it, and IBM Plex draws it as wide as a letter. These previews use ‘ (U+2018) instead. Proposal: store U+02BB, and patch a correctly spaced U+02BB glyph into each bundled font. Typed input also accepts ' and ‘.

Uzbek copy is a draft for your review. Russian and Uzbek Cyrillic layouts, the on-screen keyboard and the full state set come in the Stage 2 prototype.

Fonts load from Google Fonts for this preview only. The app bundles its fonts so it works offline.
