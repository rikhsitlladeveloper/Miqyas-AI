Three ways to design the Measure tab

All three show the same moment: the laser is connected (simulated), the last reading is 2,980 m, and three earlier readings are saved. This tab is for quick measuring without opening a project.

A huge reading on top and one large "Measure" button in thumb reach. Every reading saves itself to the list below. "Type it" moves to a small button.
