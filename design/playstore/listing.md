## Store settings (Grow users → Store presence → Store settings)

### App or game: **App**

### Category — recommended: **House & Home**; alternative: **Productivity**

- **House & Home (recommended for Play).** Play’s House & Home category is where renovation, furniture, interior and floor-plan apps live, and it is what people in Uzbekistan browse when they think “ремонт / мебель / ta’mir”. In a small country the category’s top charts are short, so a new app with a few hundred installs can appear in them — free visibility that Productivity (crowded with office, notes and e-mail apps from big companies) won’t give.
- **Productivity (alternative).** Matches the App Store choice and says “work tool”. Choose it if you want the two stores to match.
- **Not Tools.** On Play, Tools is for phone utilities (flashlights, cleaners, file managers, plain tape-measure apps). It undersells 3D, estimates and quotes.
- **Not Business.** Fits the quotes and prices, but the trades don’t browse Business for a measuring tool.
- You can change the category any time; it does not need a new release.

### Tags (up to 5, picked from Play’s list)

Play Console only lets you pick tags from its own list, which changes by category. After choosing the category, open **Manage tags** and pick the closest 5 of these, in this order: **Home improvement / Renovation**, **Interior design**, **Measurement / Measuring tools**, **Furniture**, **Floor plans / 3D design**, then **Construction** or **Business tools** if they are offered. Don’t pick tags that don’t describe the app (Play may remove irrelevant tags).
