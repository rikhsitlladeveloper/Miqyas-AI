A

Read it first

The page itself fills the screen: swipe between pages, pinch to zoom. A dark strip on top shows the total and page count. Language, rooms, views and notes move into a "Sozlash" (set up) sheet. The PDF stays as it is.

B

Checklist before sending

A short ready-to-send list: builder, customer, rooms, estimate, PDF content and language. Each row is green when ready or amber with a fix. The total is big, and the pages open from a "Ko‘rib chiqish" (review) row.

C

Your letterhead, total on page 1

The PDF changes too. Page 1 becomes a one-page quote: your name and trade as the letterhead, the customer, a short scope list, the total and the signature lines. Drawings and the detailed estimate follow. On screen, page 1 is shown large, the other pages are small tabs under it, and settings sit in a sheet as in A.
