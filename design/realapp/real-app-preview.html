What this means in use

The app keeps saving after every change, as it does now. You just stop seeing the time. If a save ever fails, the red banner "Oxirgi o‘zgarish saqlanmadi" with "Qayta urinish" still appears, because that is the one moment you need to know. On Projects, Board and Profile the empty top row goes too, so the title moves up and the list gains a row of space.

3 · KEYBOARD

The iPhone's own keyboard

The grey keyboard in the app today is drawn by the prototype. In the real app each field opens the right iPhone keyboard for what you are typing.

Three things that change with it

  1. The screen moves up by itself so the field you are typing in stays visible above the keyboard.
  2. The iPhone number pad has no minus key. Negative values are never valid in this app, so nothing is lost.
  3. The keyboard's language is the phone's, not the app's. A builder with a Russian keyboard types Russian names; the app's own texts still follow the app language.

5 · YOUR CALL

Five choices before I start

1
Which logo: A, B, C or D?

Or tell me what to change in one of them.

I'd pick A.

2
The laser, until the real meter is supported.

With the simulator gone, "take from laser" has nothing to connect to. Either the laser buttons stay and lead to "Lazer ulanmagan" (as drawn above), or they are hidden until the real meter works.

I'd keep them visible as drawn, so the screens don't change again when the meter arrives. I'd also keep the simulator in a hidden test switch for you and me only, so the confirm-a-reading flow can still be tested.

3
Sign-in, until there is a server.

Without the test code, nobody can finish creating an account, because no SMS is ever sent. The app is designed to work fully without an account.

I'd hide Create account, Log in and Google for now and open the app straight on "your name and phone". The sign-in screens stay in the code for when a server exists.

4
Android keyboard too?

You asked for the native keyboard on iPhone. The drawn keyboard would otherwise stay on Android.

I'd switch both, so the two phones behave the same.

5
How the real app is built.

The approved build plan says to rebuild everything in React Native. The other way is to keep the app you already have on the emulator and iPhone, and replace its simulated parts with real phone features inside the two native shells.

I'd keep the current app. Everything in this page fits that route, and a React Native rebuild would be a far larger job that starts from zero. One thing must change either way: photos and projects need proper storage on the phone, because the current storage holds only a few dozen photos.

After you confirm

  1. Put the chosen logo in the header, welcome screen, PDF header and both app icons.
  2. Remove "Saqlandi" and the empty header row.
  3. Switch every field to the phone's keyboard.
  4. Remove the Demo panel, labels, sample data and simulated buttons; connect the real camera, location, share sheet and backup files.
  5. Build for Android and iPhone, test on the emulator, and give you a new IPA.
