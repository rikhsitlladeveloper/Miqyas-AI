Three ways to design the room editor

All three show the same moment: Mehmonxona, 5 × 4 m, wall A confirmed (5,00), wall B received from the laser but not confirmed yet (4,012, amber), height not measured, with a door and a window placed. The wall thickness is 0,25 m, one standard brick.

The plan gets squeezedAbout eight layers sit on top of each other: mode switch, laser chip, height and thickness chips, plan, readings tray, hint line and toolbar.
The measure sheet covers the planWhen you measure, a sheet covers half the screen, so you lose sight of which wall you're on.
Small targetsThe number tags on the plan are about 26 px high: hard to hit with gloves.
No wall areasA brickmason needs each wall's area and the area minus doors and windows, but the editor shows only the floor area.
A

Big plan + bottom panel

The plan fills the screen. Tools sit on a slim rail on the right. A small panel at the bottom always shows the next step with large buttons; drag it up for the full list of measurements.
