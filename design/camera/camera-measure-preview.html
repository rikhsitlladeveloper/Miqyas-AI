Centimetres, and measuring with the camera

Three additions to the O‘lchash tab and Profile. Nothing here is built yet. The camera screen further down can be tried: draw a line on it with your finger.

1. CentimetresProfile → O‘lchov birliklari becomes a choice between Metr and Santimetr.
2. Photo with measured linesAlready exists inside a workspace. It gets a button on the O‘lchash tab and a Share action.
3. Live cameraNew screen: draw a line on the live view, measure, see the value on the line, save it as a photo.
The one limit to know first

The app cannot work out a distance from the picture. The number always comes from the laser meter, or is typed. The line you draw shows which distance the number belongs to.

"Ulashish" sends one image: the photo with its lines and numbers drawn on it. It goes through the phone's own share sheet, so Telegram, WhatsApp or any other app works.

Live camera: try it

Draw a line on the wall with your finger or mouse, then press O‘lchash. The wall here is a drawing; in the app it is the camera.

  1. Draw the line along what you are measuring. You can redraw it as often as you like.
  2. O‘lchash takes the reading from the laser. Qo‘lda lets you type it. The value appears on the line.
  3. Saqlash freezes the view into a photo with the line and value on it, and puts it under Suratlar. From there you can share it.
The line does not follow the wall

It stays where you drew it on the screen. If the phone moves, the line is no longer on the same spot. So the way to use it is: hold steady, measure, save.

In this preview the number is a sample reading, and it is the same whatever line you draw. That is the point: it does not come from the picture.

What it will not do

Measure from the pictureA phone photo has no reliable scale. AR measuring is off by centimetres and is not available in the app as it is built.
Keep the live line on the wallThat needs AR tracking. The saved photo is the lasting record.
Live video to another phoneThat needs a server and internet. Sharing the saved image works without either.
Real laser values, yetThe laser connection is still simulated, so until a real meter is supported the value is typed.

Four choices before I start

My recommendation is marked. Reply with the letters you want, for example "1A 2A 3B 4A", or just "as recommended".

1. Where is the Kamera button?
AIn the bottom row, left of Qo‘lda (shown above). Always in reach of the thumb.
BAs a sixth tool beside Maydon, Devor, Hajm, Yig‘indi, G‘isht. The row gets crowded at 360 wide.
2. Where do saved pictures go?
AUnder Suratlar on the O‘lchash tab, with "Loyihaga qo‘shish" when you want one in a project.
BAlways into a project: the app asks which workspace before saving. Slower on site.
3. How are centimetres written?
A362,5 cm: one digit after the comma, so millimetres are kept.
B363 cm: whole centimetres only. Simpler to read, but a laser reading loses its millimetres on screen.
4. Estimate other lines from one measured line?
ANo. Every line gets its own laser or typed value.
BYes, marked "taxminiy". Only roughly right, and only on a flat wall photographed straight on.
