Photo edge measuring

Part 0 mockup for builders and furniture makers. The photo editor finds straight edges and corners on the photo, line ends snap onto them, and each line still gets its value from the laser or by typing. The edge finder running here is a working prototype of the one planned for the app.

Photo

—

The three samples are drawn pictures. Try one of your own photos too: a table, cabinet, door or window.

Try it

  1. Drag from one corner of the table to the next. The end jumps onto the corner, and a loupe above your finger shows exactly where.
  2. Start on an edge and slide along it: both ends on one edge lock the line to that edge.
  3. Tap Tomonlarini ishlatish to get the length and width as lines A and B, then tap each ? to measure it.
  4. Pinch, or scroll the mouse wheel, to zoom up to 4×. Drag either end of a line to correct it.
  5. Switch off Qirralar to draw freely on a messy photo. The plain white wall shows what happens when no edges are found.

For your OK

  • Edge look: faint white lines with a thin dark shadow, so they read on light and dark photos; the edge you snap to turns blue.
  • Snap distance: about 6 mm from the finger for corners, 4 mm for edges.
  • Loupe: above the finger, moving below it near the top of the photo.
  • Letters on the value pills (A, B, C). Today pills show only the value.
  • A new line opens the value sheet straight away, as it does today. Lines from the rectangle offer don't, so you can measure them in any order.
  • The laser button gives a sample value here, as the app does until the real meter is connected.
