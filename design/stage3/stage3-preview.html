The 9 fixes

  • Typing a new value while measuring a wall again left the step card stuck.
  • The "PDF" badge covered the first words of a note.
  • Home's Note tile left an empty note behind each time.
  • A typed reading sent to a room arrived labelled "from the laser".
  • Going Back in sign-up emptied the name and phone.
  • About 30 kinds of control were under 44 px; all are 44 now.
  • Toasts covered the main button for five seconds.
  • A diagonal was called "Wall X1".
  • Deleting a project happened at once; it now asks first.

Still not checked

  • Anything that needs a real phone, laser, camera or iPhone.
  • Restore with "Replace" and "Skip".
  • The Board's "waiting N days" highlight (the test quote was sent the same day).
  • Text and eraser tools on photos and drawings.
  • Changing each value in the settings sheets.

The checklist marks each of these.

Open points that are yours to decide

The checklist lists 13 open points. Most are small and go to the build. These four need your answer.

1
Board tasks don't tick themselves.

"Measure the rooms", "Build the estimate" and "Send the quote" are ticked by hand even when the app knows they're done. I can make them tick automatically.

2
Brick sizes and spare.

You chose a stand-alone Brick tool on the Measure tab, and it is built. It starts from brick 250 × 120 × 65 mm, a 10 mm joint and 5% spare. Tell me the sizes and spare you actually use and I'll make them the defaults.

3
Wording in Uzbek Cyrillic and Russian.

The Cyrillic text is converted by machine from the Latin, and Russian is a draft. Both need a native read before the build copies them.

4
The logo.

The PDF header and the Home screen still show the METRX wordmark.
