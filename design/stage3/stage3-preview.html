The 5 fixes

  • Typing a new value while measuring a wall again left the step card stuck on "measure again".
  • The "PDF" badge covered the first words of a note on its card.
  • Home's Note tile left an empty note behind each time you opened it and went back.
  • A reading typed by hand and sent to a room arrived labelled "from the laser".
  • Going Back from the password step emptied the name and phone you had typed.

Not covered today

  • Pinch to zoom (the test tool can't pinch).

  • Confirming a restore (I stopped before it to keep the test data).
  • A 360 px wide phone on the emulator (it's 411 px; 360 px was checked in the browser).

Four things I noticed and left alone

These came up during testing. Nothing is broken, so I didn't change them without asking.

1
Board tasks don't tick themselves.

Every job starts with "Measure the rooms", "Build the estimate" and "Send the quote". The app knows when each is done, but you still tick them by hand, so a job in progress can show "Build the estimate" as its next task. I can make them tick automatically.

2
Brick and mortar calculator.

Still open from earlier. The spec says quantities are typed by hand. For brickmasons, a calculator from the net wall area would save the most time, but it changes the approved scope.

3
One laser message for three problems.

In the room editor, "Bluetooth off" and "permission denied" both show the general "Laser not connected" line. The Measure tab already names each case. Making the editor match is a small change.

4
14 controls are small for site use.

I measured every tappable control. Fourteen are under 40 px on their short side, for example "Cancel" on the measure-again card (28 px tall), "Clear" in the calculator (32 px) and the colour swatches (34 px). They all work. I'd enlarge them at the start of Stage 3 and re-check the four languages.
