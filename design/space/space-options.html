Three ways to design the workspace page

All three show the same workspace, 1-qavat (1st floor), with three rooms at different stages and three other items:

  • Mehmonxona is fully measured: 50,31 m² of net wall.
  • Yotoqxona is 1 of 3 measured, with wall B waiting for confirmation.
  • Koridor is an L-shape that isn't measured yet.
  • The other items are a photo with dimensions, a drawing and a note.
Rooms mixed with notesRoom plans, photos and notes sit in one grid, so the rooms that still need measuring don't stand out.
No totalsThe page doesn't add up the workspace's net wall area or show how much is measured.
No next stepA room card shows its area or "not confirmed", with no button to continue.
Filter chipsFour filter chips take a row and often hide just one or two items.
A

Grid by sections

The workspace totals on top, then the grid split into two labelled sections, Rooms and Photos and notes, instead of filter chips. Room cards show the measured state.
