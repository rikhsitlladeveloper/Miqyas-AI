# Miqyas AI — Business Plan (summary)

Full living version: claude.ai doc *Smart Measuring Tool + App — Business Plan for Tashkent*.

## Verdict

The drawing part (laser → phone → CAD) is commoditized: device makers ship free apps and Chinese apps cost about ¥128/year. The money is in the workflow after measuring: **measure → quote → order → install**, for trades that measure every day.

Angle: an Uzbek/Russian vertical app for measurers (замерщики) at kitchen/wardrobe makers, stretch-ceiling and window installers and renovation crews, sold as a **bundle** — own-brand Bluetooth laser + app subscription — with a company dashboard for the owner.

## Market (Uzbekistan)

- 15.9M m² of housing commissioned in 2025 (+2.8%); 7.7M m² in H1 2026 (+7.2%) — stat.uz, kun.uz
- 319.5k housing transactions in 2025 (+15.8%); mortgage lending +29% — Central Bank via kun.uz
- Each sale or new apartment triggers renovation, kitchens, wardrobes, ceilings, windows — 1–3 measuring visits each

## Competitors

| Player | Model | Gap for us |
| --- | --- | --- |
| magicplan (Canada) | $10–90 / user / month; ~$5.9M revenue 2025 | Too expensive locally; no Uzbek |
| Bosch, Leica, Stanley | Free app, sell device | Drawing only, no quotes or orders |
| 家易量 and similar (China) | ≈ ¥128 / year | Chinese only |
| МетрПлан, Замерщик (Russia) | Freemium | No Uzbek, no local prices or payments |

## Business model

| Stream | Price (assumption) | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Device | $60–80, ≈ $35 margin | First users and cash |
| Solo plan | ≈ $8 / month | Individual measurers |
| Company plan | ≈ $35–40 / month | Main income |
| Later | Developer plans, order commissions | Platform upside |

Start with **measurement-as-a-service** (paid floor plans) for cash and validation before the app is ready.

## Scaling

1. Months 0–3: validate with 10 free pilots in Tashkent; 3 agree to pay.
2. Months 3–12: furniture and ceilings, then windows; 30 paying companies.
3. Year 2: regions with most new housing; dealers in building-material markets.
4. Year 3+: Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan; developers, banks, order commissions.

## Company and funding

- Now: YaTT (sole trader) for the measuring service.
- Before first device import or employee: LLC + IT Park residency.
- Accelerators: UzCombinator once there are paying customers; Astana Hub Silkway (2027) for Kazakhstan.

## Risks

- Each device has its own BLE protocol → own-brand hardware with a documented protocol.
- Small businesses resist paying for apps → sell to owners on saved errors and staff control.
- Free OEM apps improve → moat is local workflow: language, prices, orders, payments.
- Hardware quality → test sample batches, keep spares.
- Name: "Miqyas" — a closed Saudi sizing startup and small Gulf firms use it; file the Uzbek trademark early.
